Bacteria genusoverview.

A twelve-module tour of the gut microbiome's major bacterial genera, grouped by phylum — real biology, real research citations, and real numbers pulled directly from the datasets used in this site's other two courses. Covers naming and taxonomy, diet-driven genera, butyrate producers, opportunists, a mucin specialist, and a cross-dataset comparison, closing on exactly where genus-level resolution stops being enough. Runs natively in-browser; no local installation required.
