Bacteriastrains.

A twelve-module course on the resolution level below species — where this site's running example finally gets resolved: commensal E. coli and Shiga-toxin-producing E. coli are the same species, but different strains, distinguished by specific genes. Covers real virulence and resistance genes, plasmids and mobile genetic elements, why probiotic strains aren't interchangeable, and a real cross-validated strain classifier — closing out the full genus → species → strain resolution ladder across all five courses on this site. Runs natively in-browser; no local installation required.
