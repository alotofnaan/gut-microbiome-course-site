Bioinformaticsof gut health.

An eight-module course on the computational pipeline behind gut-health tools like GMWI2 — from a raw sequencer read to a trained, cross-validated classifier. Parse real sequence data with Biopython, hand-build a presence/absence matrix, train your own Lasso logistic regression with scikit-learn, and see exactly how a 2024 published tool (Mayo Clinic's Gut Microbiome Wellness Index 2) does the same thing at real scale. Runs natively in-browser; no local installation required.
